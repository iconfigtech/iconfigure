# Project Overview

## Company profile

`iConfigure` is a Dubai-based business specializing in iPhone repair, restoration, and hardware troubleshooting. The company operates at the intersection of consumer service and professional refurbishment, supporting both individual customers and B2B partners.

## Core services

### Consumer repair

- screen replacement
- battery replacement
- housing and frame repair
- device diagnostics
- water damage recovery
- no-power / no-boot troubleshooting

### B2B and refurbishment

- device grading and resale preparation
- trade-in workflows
- return stock management
- refurbishment of repairable units
- quality sorting for resale channels

### Board-level work

- logical troubleshooting
- board-level diagnostics
- hardware fault isolation
- repair path determination before replacement

## Brand positioning

The website reflects a premium, technical, and fast-moving brand identity. The visual design communicates confidence, capability, and technical precision, which is important for a service business focused on repair and recovery.

## Business goals

The website aims to:

- establish professional brand trust
- present services clearly
- generate WhatsApp and phone leads
- support B2B inquiries
- communicate technical capability in a simple format

## Target audience

- individual customers needing repair
- local businesses and resellers
- refurbishers and device buyers
- wholesalers and trade partners
- customers seeking device restoration or second-life solutions

## Suggested next steps

For long-term growth, the project can evolve with:

1. a dedicated services page
2. a gallery of repaired devices
3. Google Maps / location details
4. testimonials and case studies
5. lead capture forms for B2B and support requests
6. an SEO-focused content layer for search visibility
7. branded assets and a consistent social identity

## Operational note

This repository is a static website and does not currently include a backend or database. It is a front-end presence designed to showcase the company and direct customers to direct contact channels.
